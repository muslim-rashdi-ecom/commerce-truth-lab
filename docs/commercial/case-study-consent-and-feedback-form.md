## 2. Case Study Publication Consent Options

Merchant explicitly selects one of the following three publication tiers by initialing:

### [ ] Option A: Full Attribution Commercial Case Study
- Provider may reference Client's corporate legal name, brand name, and public website.
- Provider may feature Client's logo in public portfolio materials.
- Specific confidential financial metrics (gross revenues, bank balance balances) remain strictly redacted; only reconciliation percentages and technical root-cause descriptions may be shared.

### [ ] Option B: Anonymized Industry Case Study *(Recommended Default)*
- Provider will NOT use Client's corporate legal name, brand name, or logo.
- The case study will use a generic industry descriptor (e.g. *"Fast-growing UAE Apparel DTC Merchant doing 8,000 monthly orders"*).
- Technical findings (e.g., CAPI deduplication failure rate, COD courier grace window delays) may be published as an educational engineering tear-down.

### [ ] Option C: Strictly Confidential / Internal Only
- All audit findings, implementation reports, and verification metrics remain 100% confidential between Client and Provider.
- No public case study, tear-down, or portfolio reference will be produced or shared.
