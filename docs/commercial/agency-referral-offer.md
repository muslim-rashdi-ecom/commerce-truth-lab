### 1. Executive Summary: Why Partner with Commerce Truth Lab?

When an agency onboards a new e-commerce client or attempts to scale paid acquisition, data discrepancies between Shopify checkouts, payment gateway payouts, and ad platforms create major relationship friction:
- If tracking is double-counting, the agency is blamed for false ROAS claims.
- If courier COD cash is leaking, the client assumes media spend is burning margin.
- If currency minor-units are miscalculated, ad platform algorithms optimize against bad purchase signals.

Commerce Truth Lab acts as an **independent, evidence-first technical verification layer**. We provide a 48-hour diagnostic sprint that establishes an unassailable data baseline before your agency launches campaigns or overhauls themes.

### 2. Partnership Models

We offer two distinct collaboration models tailored to agency operational preferences:

#### Model A: White-Label Audit Partner (Recommended for Full-Service Agencies)
- **Execution:** The agency packages the "Shopify Measurement & Funnel Truth Sprint" directly into their onboarding or monthly retainer scope.
- **Branding:** All deliverables (HTML report, PDF/Markdown executive briefing, and verification checklists) are co-branded or white-labeled with your agency’s branding.
- **Client Interaction:** We operate behind the scenes as your technical measurement and reconciliation desk. You deliver the report to the client during your onboarding presentation.
- **Turnaround:** Guaranteed 48 business hours after receiving complete sanitized CSV files.

#### Model B: Direct Referral Partner (Recommended for Fractional Leaders & Consultants)
- **Execution:** The partner introduces the merchant directly to Commerce Truth Lab for an independent audit sprint.
- **Independence:** The merchant appreciates an objective third-party audit that verifies their internal setup without agency bias.
- **Compensation:** A 20% recurring referral fee on the initial sprint and subsequent quarterly monitoring retainers.
- **Client Protection:** We sign an explicit non-compete clause ensuring we never provide theme design, media management, or custom app development.

### 4. Scope, SLA, and Deliverables

Every partner sprint includes:
1. **Audit Scope:** Up to 30-day historical transaction cohort (Shopify orders, payment captures, courier COD settlements, customer refunds, Meta CAPI/pixel events, GA4 logs).
2. **Turnaround:** Delivered within **48 business hours** of complete sanitized file upload.
3. **Forensic Deliverables:**
   - Standalone HTML interactive briefing + Markdown/JSON data exports.
   - 12 programmatic verification checks (Rules CTL-001 to CTL-012).
   - 3 Prioritized Fixes (P0 Critical, P1 High, P2 Operational) with code/configuration guidance for your technical team.
   - Evidence Reference Ledger linking every anomaly to exact transaction tokens.
   - 14-day post-fix re-verification audit to confirm that fixes held.

### 5. Data Security Boundaries & Client Confidentiality

We provide enterprise-grade data governance that protects both the agency and the merchant:
1. **Zero-PII Standard:** We strictly refuse raw customer personal data (names, email addresses, phone numbers, delivery street addresses, credit card numbers, passwords, or API tokens).
2. **Deterministic Pseudonymization:** Any internal merchant customer identifiers are cryptographically salted (SHA-256) upon ingestion.
3. **Tenant Isolation:** All audit data resides in an isolated tenant schema (`workspace_id`).
4. **Data Purge Guarantee:** All ingested CSV files and database records are permanently deleted 30 days after report acceptance.
5. **Mutual NDA:** Standard mutual non-disclosure agreement executed prior to file transfer.
