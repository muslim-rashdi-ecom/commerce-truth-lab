**Milestone Identifier:** Milestone 4  
**Product:** Commerce Truth Lab v1  
**Lead Product Architect & Auditor:** Syed Muslim Shah ([Portfolio](https://syed-muslim-shah-portfolio.vercel.app/))  
**Governance Standard:** Evidence-First, Anti-Hype, Zero Fabricated Results, Zero Raw PII / Secrets in Git.

---

### 1. Evidence Ledger Schema & Verification Policy

Every entry in this ledger documents a real merchant engagement. 
- **Rule 1:** No stage or status may be marked as complete without empirical external evidence (email confirmations, timestamps, signed PDF agreements, delivery receipts).
- **Rule 2:** Under NO circumstances may raw customer exports, plaintext PII, merchant API keys, or unredacted confidential revenue figures be committed to Git.
- **Rule 3:** Reference benchmarks and simulated tests must remain distinctly separated from live client records.

### 4. Audit Trail & Verification Checkpoints

When an external event occurs, the auditor must record the following artifacts in the project audit records (kept outside public version control):
1. **Response Proof:** Timestamped screenshot or copy of merchant email / LinkedIn reply.
2. **Discovery Call Notes:** 20-minute diagnostic notes confirming Shopify store details and scope.
3. **Signed Agreement:** Counter-signed PDF of [`docs/pilot-authorization-agreement.md`](../pilot-authorization-agreement.md) and [`docs/commercial/pilot-proposal-and-statement-of-work.md`](pilot-proposal-and-statement-of-work.md).
4. **Data Validation Receipt:** Automated ingestion logs confirming zero PII and row counts.
5. **Report Delivery Confirmation:** Sent email timestamp with HTML, Markdown, and JSON attachments within 48h.
6. **Re-Audit Ledger:** Comparison logs from the 14-day post-fix follow-up audit.
7. **Feedback Form:** Completed survey rating (1–5) and case-study release option.
8. **Invoicing Proof:** Paid invoice receipt or signed monitoring retainer contract.
