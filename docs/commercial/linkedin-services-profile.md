### 5. Supporting Services Offered

#### A. Shopify Payment and COD Reconciliation
- **Scope:** Matching Shopify orders against gateway payouts and courier remittance manifests.
- **Focus:** Detecting courier remittance lag past contractual grace windows, partial collections, shortfalls, and unremitted deliveries.
- **Ideal For:** Brands in the GCC, South Asia, or markets with significant Cash on Delivery (COD) volume.

#### B. GA4 and Meta CAPI Purchase-Signal Audit
- **Scope:** Cross-verifying browser tracking pixels and server-side Conversions API (CAPI) events against Shopify checkout orders.
- **Focus:** Detecting mismatched event IDs causing duplicate purchase reporting, missing server signals, and 100x currency minor-unit multiplication errors.
- **Ideal For:** Performance marketing teams and media agencies spending $10k+/month on paid acquisition.

#### C. Refund and Settlement Verification
- **Scope:** Reconciling total customer refunds against original order invoice authorized amounts.
- **Focus:** Flagging customer service over-refunds, duplicate adjustments, and unallocated gateway fees across multi-currency operations.
- **Ideal For:** Multi-currency luxury or DTC brands with international cross-border sales.

#### D. E-Commerce Data-Quality Audit
- **Scope:** Ingesting transactional exports into an isolated deterministic engine to assess data completeness, missing timestamps, and currency consistency.
- **Focus:** Providing an objective baseline before onboarding a new growth agency, changing attribution software, or scaling ad spend.

### 8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Do you need access to our live Shopify admin, Stripe, or Meta Ads Manager?**  
*A:* No. We intentionally operate with zero live platform logins or API tokens. You provide bounded, sanitized CSV exports covering a recent 30-day cohort. We never ask for, nor store, live store credentials.

**Q: Does our data contain customer personal information (PII)?**  
*A:* No. We provide an exact 1-page export guide detailing how to delete or exclude customer names, phone numbers, physical addresses, and payment details before uploading. Any internal identifiers are cryptographically salted (SHA-256) upon ingestion.

**Q: How quickly do we receive the audit report?**  
*A:* Complete findings and prioritized recommendations are delivered within 48 business hours after we validate that your sanitized CSV exports are complete.

**Q: Do you claim to detect fraud or recover lost revenue?**  
*A:* No. We do not make speculative claims about fraud detection or guaranteed recovered revenue. We provide deterministic mathematical proof of reconciliation variances, overdue cash collections, and tracking misalignments so your team can resolve them with certainty.
